## Server description
The NaliChat creator community: meet artists and producers, share original work, collaborate on music, talk NaliChat Studio and Video Lab, and get help using NaliChat.

## Channel categories (create in this order)

### 01 · START HERE
- **#welcome** (read-only for members) — Start here: what NaliChat is, how to join the conversation, and where to find support.
- **#rules** (read-only for members) — Our community standards and how to report problems.
- **#announcements** (read-only for members) — Verified NaliChat product releases, maintenance notices, and community events. Do not imply a release is live until confirmed.
- **#introductions** — Tell us what you make, what tools you use, and what kinds of collaborators you want to meet.

### 02 · COMMUNITY
- **#general-chat** — Casual discussions among NaliChat users and creators.
- **#share-your-work** — Share your own songs, demos, videos, artwork, and finished projects. Credit collaborators.
- **#find-collaborators** — Find musicians, vocalists, producers, designers, and video creators.
- **#creator-feedback** — Request constructive feedback and share specific improvement ideas.

### 03 · NALICHAT FEATURES
- **#studio-and-audio** — Recording, mixes, plugins, VoxShift, and Studio workflow questions.
- **#video-lab-and-art** — Music videos, cover art, and visual creation.
- **#battle-arena** — Rap battles and upcoming community challenges.
- **#app-tips** — Tips for messaging, transfers, meetings, and other NaliChat features.

### 04 · HELP & FEEDBACK
- **#help-desk** — Community troubleshooting; for account or billing information, contact official NaliChat support privately through the app.
- **#bug-reports** — Reproduction steps, device/platform, observed vs. expected behavior. Never post credentials, private account records, or payment data.
- **#feature-requests** — One idea per message with use case and expected benefit.
- **#known-issues** (staff-post-only) — Confirmed issues and their currently verified status.

### 05 · VOICE
- **Creator Lounge** — Social voice conversations.
- **Collaboration Room** — Live feedback and project planning.
- **Listening Session** — Share and discuss original or appropriately licensed music; no unauthorized recording.

### 06 · STAFF (private; only Owner/Admin/Moderator)
- **#staff-operations** — Moderation coordination and operational decisions.
- **#incident-log** — Restricted incident reports with minimal personal information.
- **#release-drafts** — Approve announcement wording before posting.

Start with fewer channels if moderation staffing is limited. Expand only when activity warrants it.

## Roles and permission boundaries
1. **Owner** — Server owner only. Enable two-factor authentication.
2. **Admin** — Trusted operations administrators; limit the Administrator permission.
3. **Moderator** — Moderate messages and manage reports; no Administrator or billing access.
4. **NaliChat Team** — Identifiable staff role, not automatically administrative.
5. **Verified Creator** — Optional creator identity badge, not an elevated permission role.
6. **Community Member** — Default member role.
7. **Premium Member** — Optional informational role only. Do not assign based on unverified Discord claims or payment screenshots; no automatic premium benefits or account linkage.

For @everyone, disable Manage Channels, Manage Roles, Manage Webhooks, Mention @everyone/@here, and Administrator. Keep staff channels invisible to @everyone. Restrict posting in #rules, #announcements, and #known-issues to authorized staff.

## Copy-ready #rules message
📜 **NaliChat Community Rules**

1. Respect others. No harassment, hate, threats, bullying, or targeted abuse.
2. Share only content you own or have permission to distribute. Credit contributors; do not impersonate artists or staff.
3. No spam, unsolicited mass DMs, phishing links, scams, deceptive promotions, or malicious downloads.
4. Keep explicit sexual content, graphic violence, and illegal content out of the community. Use channel-appropriate language and content warnings when needed.
5. Protect privacy: never publish passwords, verification codes, addresses, private conversations, payment details, or anyone's personal data.
6. Keep feedback constructive. Report bugs without leaking personal account information.
7. No unauthorized recording of voice conversations or sharing private recordings.
8. Follow Discord's Terms of Service and Community Guidelines, as well as moderator instructions.

**Need help or want to report a violation?** Use Discord's reporting tools or contact a moderator. Account/billing issues belong in official private NaliChat support, not public Discord threads.

Moderators may remove content, issue warnings, time out members, or ban accounts when necessary.

## Discord safety and launch checklist
- [ ] Confirm owner account, server name, icon, and invite destination directly in Discord.
- [ ] Enable Discord Community under Server Settings if eligible.
- [ ] Enable rules screening and an appropriate verification level.
- [ ] Enable AutoMod anti-spam and mention-spam rules; set review/alert destination to staff-only channel.
- [ ] Configure explicit media filtering and report/moderation workflow.
- [ ] Require 2FA for moderator/admin actions and keep administrative roles minimal.
- [ ] Disable broad @everyone mention permissions and staff-channel access.
- [ ] Review invite expiration/max uses before publishing widely.
- [ ] Test onboarding and posting from a normal non-staff member account.
- [ ] Post #rules, #welcome, and a first #announcements message.
- [ ] Assign at least one trusted moderator for active launch periods.
- [ ] Publish the saved NaliChat Base44 build separately **only after normal application release checks**.

## NaliChat-to-Discord boundary
Discord is an **optional community**, not a requirement for signing up, messaging, or subscribing to NaliChat. Discord role claims do not grant NaliChat Premium access. Do not ask users to log in through third-party forms or share PayPal information in channels. Discord community moderation is not a substitute for official in-app support.

## Launch measurements
Measure NaliChat 'discord_invite_click' events by source/placement. Discord membership, accepted invitations, and retention cannot be inferred from click events; use Discord's server insights or moderator observations if available. Do not advertise unsupported success metrics.
