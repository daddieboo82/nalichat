# NaliChat — Google Play Release Notes

## Version 1.0.0 (Initial Release)

### What's New

Welcome to NaliChat — the collaborative workspace for music production and industry professionals.

**Studio**
- Multi-track DAW with surgical-precision waveform editing
- Record, trim, cut, and arrange audio with click-to-select accuracy
- Real-time mixer with volume, pan, mute, and solo controls
- AI-powered mastering with genre-aware presets
- Stem splitting and bounce-to-track workflows
- Version history for every track
- Live collaborator presence in shared studio rooms

**Messages**
- Real-time 1-on-1 and group conversations
- Voice messages with in-app waveform playback
- File sharing (audio, images, documents)
- Message threads, replies, reactions, and editing
- Context-aware Nali AI assistant integrated in chat
- Content moderation with automatic policy violation detection

**Explore**
- Discover and stream original tracks, remixes, beats, and productions
- Like, comment, and share music
- Purchase tracks and files directly through the app
- Create and curate playlists
- Artist profiles with achievements and level progression

**Remix Challenges**
- Browse active challenges hosted by artists
- Download stems, submit your remix, and get voted on by the community
- Challenge leaderboards with real-time vote tracking
- Share your submissions with viral social links

**Squads**
- Pair up with a partner for weekly collaborative goals
- Unlock bonus credits by staying active together
- Track squad progress and milestones

**Nali AI Assistant**
- Proactive, context-aware AI presence throughout the app
- Generate viral content, artist bios, and cover art
- Get music production guidance and track tag suggestions
- Adjustable proactivity settings (proactive, minimal, off)

**More**
- Onboarding wizard for new users
- Leaderboard with XP and achievement system
- Analytics dashboard for tracking your growth
- Mobile-optimized with pull-to-refresh and responsive design
- Dark mode throughout

### About This Release

This is the initial public release of NaliChat on Google Play. The app is free to download and use. Create an account to start collaborating, producing, and sharing your music with the community.
